# CredResolve — Recovery Performance Investigation

## Project Objective

Investigate the claim that recovery improved by 11% month-on-month. Assess payment trends, data quality, portfolio composition, campaign-targeting associations, statistical uncertainty, and the reliability of the available recovery measures.

## Rebuild the Golden Candidates

Run these commands from the project root:

```powershell
python src/build_golden_datasets.py
python src/validate_golden_datasets.py
```

The pipeline writes candidate datasets under `data/golden/` and a pipeline summary under `reports/`. Validation results are written to `reports/golden_dataset_validation_report.csv`.

The borrower candidate intentionally preserves conflicting profiles after removing exact duplicate rows. It is not a verified one-row-per-borrower master.

## Key Findings

- Raw payments contain 25,500 rows and 500 duplicate payment-ID groups; the candidate contains 25,000 unique payment IDs.
- The payment candidate contains 368 missing payment references.
- Payment borrower IDs disagree with the account master in 24,617 candidate records.
- Borrower profile conflicts remain unresolved; do not choose an authoritative profile without a defensible rule.
- The February-to-March gross successful payment amount increased by 11.03%, while payment-level success share fell by 0.96 percentage points.
- The February-to-March account-level success share fell by 0.16 percentage points. Neither preliminary success-share test was statistically significant at the 5% level.
- The current evidence does not establish a sustained 11% improvement in underlying recovery.

## Interpretation and Limitations

The available payment status and amount fields do not independently establish verified net recovery. Currency semantics, timezone interpretation, and reliable reversal linkage remain unresolved. August is a partial month.

Targeting followed by a successful payment is an association, not proof of causal impact. Configured campaign channels are not necessarily actual contact channels. Do not use current account snapshots as if they were historical portfolio attributes.

## Recommended Decision

Do not commit the full investment based only on the observed 11% gross-amount increase. First validate recovery definitions, payment/reversal links, and entity relationships; then run a randomized account-level targeting experiment and estimate incremental net recovery after costs.
